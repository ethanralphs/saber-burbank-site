Searching "Sport Clips Burbank"? Here's the Honest Rundown

Published August 31, 2026 · 2 min read

If you typed "Sports Clips Burbank" into Google and ended up here — good, you're in the right place. We're Burbank Men's Haircuts, a Sport Clips location at 2452 W Victory Blvd. We know that's not always obvious from a search, so here's what's actually worth knowing before you make the drive over.

What we do (and what to ask for)

Men's and boys' haircuts, full stop. That covers men's cuts, kids' cuts, senior cuts — and if you haven't tried the MVP Experience yet, it's the one most regulars default to after the first time. It stacks a massaging shampoo, hot steamed towel, and neck trim on top of your haircut, and it turns a quick errand into something you don't mind having on your afternoon. We also do beard trims, ear trims, and a standalone shampoo if that's all you need.

Pricing runs $15 on the low end up through $30, $32, and $38 depending on what you're getting. Not sure what fits? Just ask at check-in — it's a two-sentence conversation.

Walk-ins, wait times, and the online check-in thing

No appointment needed, ever. Walk straight in if that's easier. But if you'd rather skip the waiting room, check in online before you leave the house — it puts you in the queue remotely, so by the time you pull into the Victory Blvd parking lot, you're already partway through the wait. On a busy Saturday, that difference is real.

Weekend mornings and the after-school window on weekdays tend to be the slowest-moving. Mid-morning on a weekday is usually the smoothest if you've got flexibility. Either way, the online queue takes most of the guesswork out of it — check in from home, or just show up. Both work fine here.
